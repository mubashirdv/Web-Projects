About Quiz Platform

Welcome to the Quiz Platform! Our goal is to provide an interactive and engaging space for students to test their knowledge across various subjects. Whether you're a guest user looking to try out some sample questions or a registered user wanting to track your progress, we've got you covered!

Features:

  • Diverse Course Categories: Explore quizzes across multiple subjects including Mathematics, Science, History, Computer Science, and Literature.
  • User-Friendly Interface: Built with Bootstrap for a responsive design that works on all devices.
  • Immediate Feedback: Get instant results and feedback on your quiz performance.
  • User Accounts: Registered users can save their scores, view their progress, and retake quizzes.
  • Guest Access: Non-registered users can browse quizzes and take sample questions without creating an account.
